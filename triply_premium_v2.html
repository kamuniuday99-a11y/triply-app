About this place
Golconda Fort is a majestic 16th-century citadel built by the Qutb Shahi dynasty. Spread across 11 km, it features 87 bastions, 8 gateways, and a breathtaking acoustic clapping system at the entrance gate — if you clap at the gate, the sound echoes to the top of the fort. The fort offers panoramic 360° views of Hyderabad from the summit. The Sound and Light Show every evening narrates its dramatic history of wars and dynasties.
Traveller Tips
Arrive before 9 AM on weekdays to avoid heavy crowds. Weekends can get very crowded by 10 AM.
Buy tickets only from the ASI (Archaeological Survey of India) counter — never from touts outside. Official price is ₹35 for Indians.
Wear comfortable shoes — there are 380 steps to the top. Carry water, especially in summer (Apr–Jun can reach 42°C).
Best months: October to February. Avoid national holidays when the fort is extremely crowded.
